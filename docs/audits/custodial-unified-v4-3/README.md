## Retained product doctrine

The audits consistently support retaining:

- private Android employee product;
- four-button Employee Home: Schedule, Messages, Events and Feedback;
- enrolled-device employee identity;
- ambient NFC with no normal Scanner or QR workflow;
- assigned areas, not a phone-directed walking route;
- static schedules as normal authority;
- individual location as final ownership unit;
- separate responsible owner, active cleaner and actual cleaner;
- service occurrence as the root of due/overdue/satisfaction/correction;
- explicit `OPEN` and `not_required` states;
- Event information separated from approved operational impact;
- CoverAll as contractor capacity rather than employee identity;
- Read Only as a separate private Dashboard/Events product;
- exact two-cycle notification audio and stale-visible cancellation;
- analytics anti-misuse and confidence/dispute treatment;
- Build 22 retained until a later bound physical rollback is proved.
